## Purpose

This brief captures the verified structure and tone of the prior Kybalion meeting invites so the next meeting can be planned from real project history instead of memory.

## Verified Prior Pattern

### Quick invite pattern

The short-form invite used a simple outreach structure:

- Friendly opener: "Hi friends,"
- Broad framing around "learning and applying universal law and principles"
- Low-pressure cadence: "once or twice a month"
- Practical positioning: "light reading and real conversation" and "practical takeaways you can use"
- Clear lightweight RSVP: reply with "I'm in"
- Full details deferred to the main Kybalion hub

### Session 1 full invite pattern

The Session 1 invite established the main long-form template:

- Title format: `Session N, [Day], [Month] [D], [YYYY] @ [time] [TZ]`
- Series label: `Universal Laws & Principles, The Kybalion`
- Warm, non-academic tone
- Reading scope called out explicitly
- Two short utility sections:
  - `What to expect`
  - `How to prepare`
- One study-guide CTA block
- Close with the Kybalion hub link

Session 1 covered:

- Introduction
- Chapter 1, `The Hermetic Philosophy`

### Session 2 full invite pattern

The Session 2 invite shifted from initial outreach to continuation/follow-up:

- Thanks and recap of the prior meeting
- Link back to the previous session study guide
- Explicit statement of the next reading assignment
- Google Calendar CTA block near the top
- New study-guide CTA block for the upcoming session
- Short prep checklist at the end

Session 2 covered:

- Chapter 2, `The Seven Hermetic Principles`

### Pattern notes worth preserving

- The tone is invitational, practical, and conversational rather than scholarly.
- Each invite keeps the reading scope narrow and concrete.
- The copy consistently translates the text into everyday-life application.
- The main CTA pattern is: review prior session, prepare for next session, then use the hub or calendar link.

## Drafting Guidance

Use these rules for the next invite draft:

1. Open by thanking attendees for Session 2 and briefly naming what was covered.
2. Introduce Session 3 as the next step in the study sequence.
3. Name the chapter explicitly: `Mental Transmutation`.
4. Translate the concept into plain language, such as shifting inner states, redirecting thought patterns, or applying Hermetic ideas in daily life.
5. Keep prep instructions short and behavioral:
   - read once straight through
   - reread slowly
   - bring one question and one real-life example
6. Include placeholders for:
   - date and time
   - Zoom or meeting location
   - calendar invite link
   - Session 3 study guide PDF
7. Keep the closing CTA simple: calendar, study guide, or Kybalion hub.
